Where to get help with your servers, and who to write to for everything else.

Help with your servers

Ask in the plugged.host Discord server. The people who run plugged.host are there, and so are many server owners who have solved the same problem before. There are no private support tickets, so keep passwords, tokens and personal data out of public channels.

For anything about your own servers, neo can usually help faster: open neo in the panel and describe the problem. It can read your logs, change settings and fix what it finds. The guides cover the common tasks step by step.

Is something down?

Check status.plugged.host first. It shows the website, the panel and every node, refreshed every minute, along with open incidents. You can follow incidents over RSS.

Reviews

Tell other players how plugged.host works for you, good or bad, in a review on Trustpilot, and read what others wrote on our Trustpilot page. A review is not a support request: for help, ask in Discord.

legal@noprofit.space. Report a security problem privately and do not post it in Discord. Write to us before testing anything; security research needs our written permission first.
