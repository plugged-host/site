Share a server with friends

Running a server together? Give your friends their own access instead of your password, with only the permissions they need.

Updated 12 October 2026

Add a subuser

  1. Ask your friend to sign in to panel.plugged.host once, so their account exists, and to tell you the email address of their noprofit.space account.
  2. On your server, open Users and choose Invite User.
  3. Enter their email address and tick the permissions they should have.

The server then shows up in their own server list. They sign in with their own account, so you never share your password, and you can remove their access at any time.

Choose permissions

Permissions are grouped by area. A few common setups:

A moderator
Console and power control, so they can run commands and restart the server.
A builder or developer
Files and SFTP, so they can upload worlds, plugins or code. Add startup if they need to change the server's version or main file.
A co-owner
Everything, including backups, schedules, databases and users. Only for people you trust as much as yourself.

plugged.host adds two permissions of its own: Transfer lets them move the server to another location, and neo lets them use neo on it.

The server's resources still come from your own account's pool, not your friends'.

See who did what

Every action on the server lands in its activity log: who started or stopped it, edited which file, ran which command, made or restored a backup, and what neo did. When something changes unexpectedly, look there first. Subusers can see it if you give them the activity permission.
