Your resources, credits, tokens and Plus

Every account gets one free pool of CPU, memory and disk to share between its servers. Here is how it works, and how to get more without paying.

Updated 28 October 2026

The pool

Your account's CPU, memory and disk are one budget for all your servers together. Spend it on one large server or spread it over several small ones; there is no limit on the number of servers, only on the pool. Today every account gets:

  • CPU: 400%, about four threads.
  • Memory: 12 GB, or 16 GB while the Discord account linked to your noprofit.space account is in the plugged.host Discord server.
  • Disk: 50 GB.

Each server can also have up to five ports, five databases and five backups. These numbers are a ceiling, not a guarantee: allocations depend on available capacity and can change as the fleet does, as the Terms explain.

Plans

When you create a server, a plan fills in its size: Starter (100% CPU, 2 GB memory, 10 GB disk), Standard (200%, 4 GB, 20 GB), Pro (300%, 8 GB, 40 GB), Ultimate (400%, 12 GB, 50 GB) and larger ones if your pool allows. You can also type your own values. To resize a server later, ask neo.

Start small. A server that uses less of the pool leaves room for your next one, and most servers need less than people expect.

Credits: more resources

Credits come from the plugged.host Discord server:

  • Invites: invite a friend, and you earn credits once they have stayed for a few hours. Their Discord account has to be older than a month, and the credits are taken back if they leave.
  • Boosts: boosting the server earns more.
  • Giveaways in the Discord server.

Spend them in the Resource Store in the panel on more CPU, memory and disk for your pool. What you buy is yours to keep. The first steps are cheap and each costs a little more than the last, so the store stays fair for everyone. Your progress is on the Discord rewards tab of your profile.

Tokens: Plus, Premium and extras

Tokens come from the Tokens page in the panel, in two ways:

  • A survey from CPX Research, once a day. It is the best-paying way: each survey pays what it shows.
  • Sponsored links, if you prefer: each link you complete pays a little.

Tokens pay for plugged.host Plus and Premium, for access to the panel itself (5 tokens for 30 days; Plus and Premium include it), and for extras such as subdomains.

Plus and Premium

plugged.host Plus
15 tokens for 30 days. Plus locations on hardware never shared with the free tier, priority in the start queue when a node is busy, no banners, popups or in-game ads, each server bursting up to +100% CPU while its node has room, faster archives, unpacking and backups, VS Code on your server (2 vCPU, 2 GiB RAM, 5 GiB disk), panel access, and a quarter more neo usage.
plugged.host Premium
30 tokens for 30 days, with everything Plus gives, so you never need both. Plus and Premium locations, no ads anywhere, the highest priority in the start queue, each server bursting up to +200% CPU, the fastest archives, unpacking and backups, a bigger VS Code devbox (4 vCPU, 4 GiB RAM, 10 GiB disk), panel access, and a quarter more neo usage than Plus.

Nothing renews by itself: buying again before one ends adds to the time left. When one ends, servers on locations it opened move to a location you can still use, and your servers burst only as far as what you still have allows.

Starting a server

plugged.host is paid for by ads, so starting a server shows a short ad page first, Ad-funded start, for up to half a minute; starts in the next ten minutes skip it. Premium skips it always; with Plus it still shows. When a node is very busy, starts wait in a short queue, Waiting to start, and the server starts by itself when there is room.

Idle servers

A server with no activity in the panel for two days is stopped to free capacity for others; start it again whenever you like. If it stays idle longer, it can be moved into hibernation, and its Console then offers Restore server, which brings it back for a few tokens. A server that stays hibernated for a week is deleted. This applies to every server, with or without Plus or Premium.
