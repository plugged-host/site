Guides/Using the panel

Get things done with neo

neo is the assistant built into the plugged.host panel. Tell it what you want in plain words, and it does the work on your server instead of explaining how.

Updated 28 October 2026

Start a conversation

  1. Open the panel and choose Ask neo, in the corner of the dashboard and of every server's pages.
  2. The first time, read Before you start and choose Got it, start. If your account isn't connected yet, choose Activate neo.
  3. Type what you want. When you are on a server's page, neo knows which server you mean.

What to ask

neo reads your server's console, files and settings, and can act on them. Some things it does well:

  • "Install LuckPerms and EssentialsX."
  • "Switch this server to Fabric 1.21.1 and install the Fabulously Optimized modpack."
  • "My server crashes on start, find out why and fix it."
  • "Set the difficulty to hard and the view distance to 8."
  • "Back up the server, then delete the nether."
  • "Create a new Python server for my bot with 2 GB of memory."

When the log check on a Minecraft server's console finds a problem, Ask neo to fix it hands the problem straight to neo.

You approve the risky parts

By default, neo asks before it edits or deletes files, runs console commands, or starts and stops a server. Reading never needs approval. You see exactly what it wants to do and choose whether it goes ahead.

If you trust it with routine changes, open neo settings (the gear in neo's window) and turn on Let neo make changes without asking. Even then, restoring a backup, deleting a server and spending your tokens always wait for your approval. Enable neo in the same place turns it off completely.

Limits

neo is free. Fair-use limits apply, counted over a five-hour window and a weekly window; when you reach one, neo says so and tells you when it is available again. plugged.host Plus raises both by a quarter, and Premium by another quarter on top.

Good to know

  • neo is an AI. It is usually right but not always, so read what it proposes before you approve it, and keep backups.
  • Your messages, and the server information neo reads to answer them, are processed by AI model providers; the Privacy Policy explains how. Don't paste passwords or tokens into the chat.
  • Subusers can use neo on a shared server only if you give them the neo permission.
